Each tool does one back‑office job properly.

Small, focused apps for commerce compliance and bookkeeping. Nothing else bolted on.

Export für DATEV and EU Compliance Kit are Wix apps (with Shopify versions), one is a Shopify app, one is a web app for German tax advisors, one is a free WooCommerce plugin. Each has its own site, support address, privacy policy and terms.

  • Export für DATEV

    Orders and refunds as a monthly DATEV posting batch (EXTF) for the merchant's German tax advisor. SKR03/SKR04, OSS, refunds, gift cards, and the quarterly OSS return as an upload file for the BZSt portal. Runs inside the Wix dashboard or the Shopify admin — one click per month, no file to move. In the Wix App Market · in the Shopify App Store · how it differs from Buchungsstapel.

  • EU Compliance Kit

    Five EU rules on the product page: Omnibus 30-day prior price, the withdrawal button, the EU legal guarantee notice, GPSR safety details and the GARAN durability label. Free plan for the first three. In the Wix App Market.

    Typeapp · Shopify app LanguageEnglish, German HostingEU Siteeucompliance.justarobot.online InstallAdd to Wix

  • Buchungsstapel

    For German tax advisors: clients upload their shop's monthly order export (CSV) through a link, the DATEV posting batch (EXTF) arrives by mail with every gap flagged. Nothing stored. Kanzlei plan for many clients; further shop platforms on request. The same posting batch as Export für DATEV, but from the file instead of the shop: no store access, any platform, any past month.

    TypeWeb app LanguageGerman HostingEU Sitebuchungsstapel.de

  • Buchungsstapel Order Export

    WooCommerce has no order export in core. This free plugin adds one: pick a month under WooCommerce → DATEV-Export, download the CSV that Buchungsstapel turns into the posting batch. No settings, no database tables, nothing leaves the shop. What it exports, in German.
