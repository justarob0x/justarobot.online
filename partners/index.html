Questions

  • Does my client pay more?No. The price is the one on the App Store listing. Your share comes out of what we receive.
  • What about shops on a free plan?A free plan has no payment, so there is no share. If the shop moves to a paid plan later, your share starts then.
  • Which shops count as mine?Shops you tell us about within 30 days of their install that were not already paying us and were not already registered by another partner.
  • Can a merchant join for their own shop?No. The programme is for people who advise or build for other businesses. We do not pay merchants to install an app, and we never pay for reviews.
  • Which tax forms do you need?We are a US company. Partners in the United States send a Form W-9. Partners elsewhere send a Form W-8BEN or W-8BEN-E. You are responsible for your own taxes on what we pay you.
  • Do I have to be a Shopify Partner?No. You only need clients who run a Shopify shop.
  • Can I leave?Any time, by email. Shares for payments we received up to that day are still paid.
